Revisit on (T4.2) – sketch, screen 17

Indicative, not a pixel specification. Screen texts are Dutch, as in the app (app_nl.arb). Sample data is fictional; today is Friday 9 October 2026. Orange outline = new. Already decided (PO, 04-10-2026; REQ-021, DD-005): Terugzien op… ("Revisit on…") parks an item: its status becomes Gepland (scheduled) with a date, a day like a deadline. From the start of that day the app puts it back to inbox: at start-up, when the app comes back to the front and at a change of day while it runs. A notification cannot change the encrypted database, so this check is the mechanism itself; the notification is the signal. Changing a parked item's status by hand drops its date. Where to park (a): the item detail, triage, the step Stilgevallen of the weekly review and Gepland in the status chip. The choice (b): Morgen, Volgende week (Monday), Over een maand, Kies datum…. The notification (c): in the one notification a day. Vandaag (d): a group "Terug in je inbox". Windows (e): returning works; notifications follow in T4.5. No inline token (f).

✎ Kies datum…
  • Returning: status Gepland with a date → inbox from the start of that day, written as a normal change (HLC, change log), so two devices that both do it agree. The inbox is otherwise never a status to move back to (DD-018 point 1); this is a repository method of its own, like classify in triage.
  • The date stays after returning: it marks the item for the group on Vandaag while it is in the inbox. Only Gepland with a date returns, so a processed item never returns twice. Changing a parked item's status by hand clears the date.
  • A deadline before the date is allowed: the deadline still shows on Vandaag and gets its reminder, whatever the status.
  • Volgende week is next Monday (on a Monday the Monday after); Over een maand the same day next month, or its last day.
