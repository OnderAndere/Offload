Links between items (T10.9) – sketch, screen 14

Indicative, not a pixel specification. Screen texts are Dutch, as in the app (app_nl.arb); typeface IBM Plex Sans. Sample data is fictional. Orange outline = new. Already decided (PO, 03-10-2026; AC REQ-054, advice of the architecture agent 27-09-2026): [[Tekst]] stays readable in the text, the link itself is stored on the UUID of the target (ItemLink), so renaming breaks nothing; removing [[…]] from the text removes the link. (a) a target in the trash keeps its link, the chip says so; a renamed target shows its new title, the text keeps what you typed; (b) [[Iets]] never creates an item by itself: typed without choosing it links only when exactly one item has that first line, otherwise the chip says "geen item", and in the item detail a tap creates a new inbox item with the link; (c) Koppelen in the duplicate hint writes [[…]] into the text of the newest of the two. Were open for the PO: (d)–(g).
Decided (PO, 03-10-2026): (d) a section Verwijst hiernaar · n below the buttons; (e) 🔗 plus the current title in the calm accent colour; (f) about 60 characters; (g) yes, a button [[ item in the prefix bar. See DESIGN.md, DD-004 point 13 and DD-018 point 14.

  1. Trash and renaming (decided): the link stays; the chip says "in prullenbak" and opens the item with Terugzetten; items in the trash are not listed under Verwijst hiernaar. Renamed: the chip shows the new title; the text keeps what you typed (the app never changes your text).
  2. No item yet (decided): nothing is created by itself; typed without choosing, [[…]] links when exactly one item has that first line; otherwise "geen item", and in the item detail a tap creates a new inbox item with the link.
  3. Duplicate hint (decided): Koppelen writes [[…]] into the text of the newest item.
  4. Backlinks (decided): a section Verwijst hiernaar · n (EN "Linked from") below the buttons, only when there are any; items in the trash left out. Rejected: folded, like Genoemd in on a person.
  5. Link chip (decided): 🔗 plus the current title, in the calm accent colour; no chip on cards (the card shows the text without brackets).
  6. Inserted text (decided): the first line of the target, cut at a word boundary after about 60 characters with "…". Shorter is calmer in the text; the chip always shows the full current title.
  7. A sixth button in the prefix bar (decided) [[ item, so the syntax is visible (REQ-034). The bar gets longer; it already scrolls sideways.
