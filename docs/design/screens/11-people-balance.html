People with commitment balance (T10.2) – sketch, screen 11

Indicative, not a pixel specification. Screen texts are Dutch, as in the app (app_nl.arb). Already decided (PO, 02-10-2026; AC REQ-048, DD-015, DD-017): two totals at the top; per person a bar from a central axis, left orange what I deliver, right blue what I wait for, the count in the bar; the age of the oldest open item, marked from a threshold on (21 days by default, Settings "Oud na"); people with open items first, most open items (deliver + wait) on top, alphabetical when equal, then the others subdued, alphabetical; a tap opens the person as the 1-on-1 preparation (the existing person screen: late and oldest first, date and project on the cards, the note on top); on Windows the person opens in the right pane next to the list. Age: what I deliver since it was asked, what I wait for since it was captured. Sample data is fictional. Orange outline = new. Open for the PO were (a) to (d), in the notes and the legend.
Decided (PO, 02-10-2026): (a) to (d) all as proposed; (b) to be judged again after use; (c) "Oud na N dagen" means N days or older. See DESIGN.md, DD-018 point 11.
