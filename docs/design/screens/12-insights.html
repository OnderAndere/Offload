Insights (T10.7) – sketch, screen 12

Indicative, not a pixel specification. Screen texts are Dutch, as in the app (app_nl.arb); typeface IBM Plex Sans, numbers and week numbers in IBM Plex Mono (DD-017). Already decided (PO, 02-10-2026; AC REQ-052, DD-017, SPEC Q27): the tab is called Inzicht (EN "Insights"), the fifth destination (Ctrl+5); segments Horizon, Stilstand and Personen in v1 (Verbanden comes with T10.14, after v1); orange = needs my attention now, blue = calm, the difference between delivering and waiting in the shape; a week is busy from 5 open items with a deadline in it (what I deliver only; Settings "Druk vanaf" 3–15); Inzicht is the first step of the weekly review; IBM Plex for the whole app. Sample data is fictional; today is Friday 2 October 2026 (week 40). Orange outline = new. Open for the PO were (a) to (f), in the notes and the legend.
Decided (PO, 02-10-2026): (a) to (f) all as proposed.
