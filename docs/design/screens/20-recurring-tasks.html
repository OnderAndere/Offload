Recurring tasks (T4.7, REQ-061) – sketch, screen 20

Indicative, not a pixel specification. Screen texts are Dutch, as in the app (app_nl.arb). Sample data is fictional; today is Tuesday 6 October 2026. Orange outline = new. Decided (PO, 06-10-2026; SPEC Q29–Q35, AC REQ-061, DD-018 point 18): the repetition is a token in the text (!dagelijks, !wekelijks, !maandelijks; EN !daily, !weekly, !monthly; recognised in every app language), for a Taak or an item without a type yet, fixed on the calendar. A date token in the same text is the first time of the series; without one, the first time done, else the day the token was typed. Completing a time records it on the item ("Gedaan") and moves the item to the next date of the series after today; a time left for days is one overdue card. Model B (one series item plus a record per time) was chosen; the table below shows the comparison with model A.

2 · Vandaag: one card per series

A repeating task is on Vandaag on its date, like any deadline. Left for days it is one overdue card (the run of 29 Sep), never a pile. The card has a calm chip ↻ wekelijks. Swiping right completes this time: the card leaves, and the message names the next time. Undo takes the record back and puts the item back on its old date and status.

4 · A task without repetition: "Afronden en herhalen…"

For a task that does not repeat yet, ⋮ has Afronden en herhalen…. The sheet shows the next time per rhythm, counted from the task's date (or today). A choice adds the token to the end of the text (a user edit, as a button does), completes this time and moves the item on, in one step with undo. Also for an item without a type; not for an idea, request or note.

5 · Stopping a series

Three ways, all keep the history. Remove the token from the text: the item is an ordinary task again, with the date of its text. ⋮ → Herhaling stoppen: removes the token and completes the item in one step, with undo; it then sits under Afgerond in its project, with its "Gedaan" list. Afgerond or Gearchiveerd in the status chip ends the series too.

6 · Not a task

The token works on a Taak and on an item without a type yet (from the widget). On an idea, request or note its chip says so and the item does not move on when completed. The date stays the one the series had reached, so changing the type never makes an item jump back to an old date; making it a Taak again resumes the series.

7 · Elsewhere (no new screens)

Everything below follows from the item's date, which is always the current time of the series.

Where a repeating task shows
Vandaag: on its date and overdue, one card
Melding: a line in the notification of every series date, also while a time is open
Horizon: only the next time, one dot per series

Stilstand: completing a time counts as a touch
Zoeken, archief: one item, as now
Windows: Ctrl+Enter completes this time; ⋮ as on the phone

Q32 · One item per time (A) or one series item with a record per time (B)

A · a new item per time (PO's first preference)B · one series item + a light record per time (chosen)
HistoryEvery time is a done item of its own: "when did I do it" is the list of done copies, spread over Afgerond in the project.The item shows Gedaan: date, on time or late. All in one place.
Text as the only sourceThe new item needs the next date in its text: the app must write a new date token into a copy (!di → !13-10-2026), or the date comes from a column besides the text.The text never changes. The date is worked out from the text and the records: the first time series date after the last done one.
Archive and projectsA daily task gives 365 done items a year under Afgerond.One item.
Search indexEvery copy is indexed: "hardlopen" gives a hit per time.One hit.
InsightsHorizon and Standstill see only the open copy, so fine; a later "how often did I do it" has to collect copies by series.Horizon: the next time. Standstill: completing touches the item. A later streak or count reads the records directly.

Sync (Q33)The next item gets UUIDv5(series, date): two devices make the same row.The record gets UUIDv5(item, date): two devices make the same record and work out the same next date; no item is ever made twice.
UndoTrash the new item (tombstone) and put back the old status.Remove the record (tombstone) and put back the old date and status.
A note per time ("5 km")Possible: each copy has its own note.Not in v1; a record can get a short note later.
  • Series dates are calendar dates (LocalDate), never times: summer and winter time do not exist for them; the reminder of a day comes at the day's set time, as now.
  • Monthly counts from the first time's day of the month: on the 31st it falls on the last day of shorter months (30 Nov, 28 Feb, 29 Feb in a leap year) and goes back to the 31st after (31 Dec), never drifting to the 28th. A first time on 29 Feb repeats monthly on the 29th (28 Feb in other years).
  • Next time after completing = the first series date after both the completed time's date and today: early (Saturday for Tuesday) gives the Tuesday after; late (Thursday for Tuesday) gives the next Tuesday; a daily task left three days gives tomorrow.
