Photos in an item (T5.3) – sketch, screen 23

Indicative, not a pixel specification. Screen texts are Dutch, as in the app (app_nl.arb). Sample data is fictional; today is Saturday 10 October 2026. Orange outline = new. Decided before the sketch (PO 10-10-2026, a–f): the camera through the device's camera app, its file in Offload's own cache removed at once (a); one photo at a time (b); deleting asks first, no undo (c); tapping a photo opens it full screen with zoom, the rest of viewing in T5.6 (d); photos only in the item detail, not while capturing (e); business cards later (f). A photo goes through the pipeline of T5.2: at most 1600 px, upright, without EXIF and location, encrypted; the thumbnail is 256 px.

1 · The item with photos (Android)

A · Photos under the note, the button with the others

A row of thumbnails under Notitie, oldest first (the order of a whiteboard session), only when the item has photos. More than fit: the row scrolls sideways. Foto toevoegen is an outlined button next to Wachten op… and Waar was ik?, for every item, also a done one.

B · Adding: camera or gallery

On Android the button opens a sheet with two choices. Foto maken opens the camera app; after OK there the photo comes in. Uit galerij opens Android's own photo picker (no permission). Cancelling either changes nothing.

C · While the photo is made ready

At the end of the row a tile Foto verwerken… (about 1–1.5 s for a 50 MP photo). The screen stays usable: typing, scrolling, going back. The tile becomes the thumbnail when done. A photo Offload cannot read (e.g. a HEIC on Windows) gives a message and keeps nothing.

2 · Viewing and deleting

D · Full screen, with zoom

A tap on a thumbnail opens the photo on black: pinch or double tap to zoom, drag to move. In the bar the way back and 🗑 Verwijderen. One photo at a time: the next is a tap on its thumbnail. PDFs and Openen in andere app come with T5.6.

4 · Windows

H · The same in the detail pane; the button opens the Open dialog

No camera on Windows: Foto toevoegen… opens Windows' own Open dialog with the filter Afbeeldingen (jpg, jpeg, png, webp, gif, bmp; also Alle bestanden). Thumbnails take focus with Tab: Enter opens, Del asks to delete. In the viewer Esc closes and the mouse wheel zooms.

Notes

  • Where: only the item detail (decision e); not the capture screen, the widget or triage. Cards in the lists get no photo mark in this task; thumbnails on cards come with T5.4.
  • One at a time (decision b): the button is dimmed while a photo is made ready, so two never run together.
  • Camera (decision a, Android): the camera app writes its JPEG into Offload's own cache (cache/camera/); Offload reads it, removes it at once and removes any leftover at start. Some camera apps also keep a copy in their own gallery; Offload cannot prevent that.
  • Gallery (Android): Android's photo picker, one photo; the bytes are read into memory, no copy on disk.
  • Thumbnails come from the encrypted thumbnail file through Offload's own image provider with the attachment id as cache key (DD-006 point 5), pulled forward from T5.4.
  • Accessibility: each thumbnail reads "Foto 2 van 3"; the button and the menu entries are normal buttons (≥ 48 dp).
