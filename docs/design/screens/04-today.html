(item detail, DD-018 point 1)
  • What is on Today: every live item that is not done or archived and whose deadline (due_at, from the first date in the text, DD-018 point 1) is today or earlier; any status, the inbox included. Items without a deadline are not here. Later: "revisit on" items (M4) and waiting-for items past their expected date (T10.1).
  • Order: oldest deadline first. The time since capturing is left out here: the deadline is what counts.
  • The inbox line: the number of inbox items and Verwerken, which starts triage (DD-018 point 3); tapping the line itself goes to the Inbox tab. Hidden when the inbox is empty.
  • Colours: an overdue or today date is orange with its own icon (DD-017); the swipe to complete is blue (calm, done), the trash grey.
  • Until navigation exists (T2.6) the app keeps opening on the inbox; Today comes with the navigation.
