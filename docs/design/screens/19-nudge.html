Waiting nudge (T10.1, the nudge) – sketch, screen 19

Indicative, not a pixel specification. Screen texts are Dutch, as in the app (app_nl.arb). Sample data is fictional; today is Friday 9 October 2026. Orange outline = new. Decided (PO, 04-10-2026; AC REQ-047, DD-005, DD-015): a waiting item without an expected date that has not been touched for X days (7 by default, "Navragen na") is "silent": it shows on Vandaag in a group "Blijft stil" and gets one notification on the day it becomes silent, at the time of the day's reminder (its own notification, like birthdays; a switch under Herinneringen). Ontvangen completes it (with undo); Nagevraagd counts as a touch, so the period starts again. An item with an expected date is handled by that date ("Had binnen moeten zijn"), never also by silence. Windows: the same, through the tray.

1 · Vandaag: the group "Blijft stil"

Under "Had binnen moeten zijn". Each card says whom it waits for and how long it has been silent, with Ontvangen and Nagevraagd. Swiping right is received, as elsewhere on Vandaag. After Nagevraagd the card leaves the group, with a short message; no undo, as Houden in the weekly review (putting the moment back would be a touch too; revised while building).
