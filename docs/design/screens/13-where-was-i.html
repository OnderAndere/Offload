"Where was I" (T10.3) – sketch, screen 13

Indicative, not a pixel specification. Screen texts are Dutch, as in the app (app_nl.arb); typeface IBM Plex Sans. Already decided (PO, 02-10-2026; AC REQ-049): a compact form with two fields, Volgende stap and Wat ik in mijn hoofd had, the focus in the first, Enter saves and Shift+Enter is a new line, one filled field is enough, two empty ones save nothing, the form loads no lists (b); one current note per item or project, updated while open, resumed ones kept in the background (c); a resume card at the top of the item or project with both fields and how long ago, Hervat closes it, Bijwerken opens the form filled in, the card stays until resumed (d); not on Today for now (e); taking one counts as touching the item (f). Sample data is fictional. Orange outline = new. Open for the PO were (a) and (b′).
Decided (PO, 02-10-2026): (a) variant B, the button by Afronden (C stays the alternative), and on Windows Ctrl+Shift+P; (b′) closing with text saves it anyway (discard with Hervat, or empty the fields). See DESIGN.md, DD-018 point 13.
