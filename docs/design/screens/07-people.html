Behaviour and the open points

  • The three lists. Gevraagd door: items whose requester is this person, in any status. Wacht op: items that wait on this person; open = status Wacht op, Afgerond = the done ones that waited on them. One item can be in both (Jan asked for it and you wait on Jan). Genoemd in: items that name the person with @ (also through an alias) and are in neither of the two others. Every list: open items first, Afgerond folded with its count; archived items and the trash not shown. Order: overdue and dated items by deadline, then the rest newest first.
  • Counts on the list (✋, ⏳, ⚠) count open items as in Projects: not done, not archived, not a note. Genoemd in is not counted on the list.
  • Renaming: the new name must not be empty and not be the name or an alias of another living person. Renaming to one of the person's own aliases swaps them. Through the change log, in one transaction (DD-015).
  • Keys on Windows: in the people list ↑/↓ and Enter opens; in a person the list keys of DD-018 point 5 apply (Del, Ctrl+Enter). Ctrl+4 goes to Personen. The order of DD-011 stays: once Overzicht is built (T10.7) it comes before Projecten.
  • Capturing while a person is open stays plain capture: to the inbox, without a person (REQ-001).
  • (a) A filter field at the top of the list, by name and alias (proposal: yes; with dozens of people scrolling gets slow). Search itself keeps finding items, not people.
  • (b) Where a tap on @Jan in the item detail goes. Proposal: phone the person opens over the item, back returns to the item; Windows the list pane switches to Personen › Jan and the detail pane keeps the item. A name that is not saved yet (no person yet) does nothing until the text is saved.
  • (c) The requester and the person waited on as a chip on cards elsewhere (proposal: yes, unless the text already names them).
  • (d) Genoemd in unfolded after the two lists (proposal), or folded.
  • (e) Renaming to the name of someone else: refused with a message for now (proposal); merging comes in T3.2b.
  • Later: merging (T3.2b), the profile (M10, DD-012), commitments and the balance bars (T10.1, T10.2).
