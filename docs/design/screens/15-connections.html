Connections (T10.14) – sketch, screen 15

Indicative, not a pixel specification. Screen texts are Dutch, as in the app (app_nl.arb); typeface IBM Plex Sans. Sample data is fictional; today is Sunday 4 October 2026 (week 40). Orange outline = new. The reference sketch overview/04-connections.png (design v1) is the starting point; after this sketch (PO 04-10-2026): 2 steps at most 8 direct and 8 second-step nodes, and the graph can be zoomed (DD-018 point 15); where they differ, this sketch and DD-018 point 15 win. Already decided (PO, 04-10-2026; AC REQ-059, DD-017): the fourth segment of Inzicht, NL Verbanden, EN Connections; a local graph around one focus, 1 stap or 2 stappen, never a global graph. Nodes are items, projects and people (no tags in v1); lines are [[…]] links in both directions (items in the trash left out, done items shown with a tick), an item and its project, a sub-project and its parent, an item and its people (@person, asked by, waiting for). Beslissing and vraag come with T10.11/T10.12 (after v1). A fixed radial layout, drawn by the app itself (no package): at most 12 direct nodes, on the second step at most 2 per direct node and 16 in all; the rest is "+n meer". The start focus is the item last opened (while the app runs), otherwise an empty state; entries: Inzicht, and Verbanden tonen in an item, a person and a project; not in the weekly review; the list form only for screen readers and behind "+n meer". Decided (PO, 04-10-2026): (a)–(e) all as proposed; see DESIGN.md, DD-018 point 15.

1 · Verbanden, 1 stap

The focus in the middle, its direct connections around it, each with a shape, a type word and the title (two lines at most). Shapes follow the type icons of the app: an item a square with its type icon (a task done: ticked), a person a circle with initials, a project a filled block. (a) An item due today or overdue gets the orange outline and "vandaag" / "verlopen" in its type line, as on the cards. Tap = new focus; long press = preview.

Windows

(e) With two panes the graph sits in the list pane and the right pane shows the focus (item detail, person or project), as with Personen. The mouse pointer on a node shows the preview as a card; a click focuses. Keys: Tab reaches the focus line, the depth switch and the graph; in the graph ←→↑↓ select the nearest node in that direction, Enter focuses, Space shows the preview, Esc or Alt+← goes back to the previous focus.

  1. Today and overdue (decided): an item due today or overdue gets the orange outline and "vandaag" / "verlopen" in its type line, the same rule as the date chip (DD-017: orange = needs my attention now). Without it the graph has no orange at all.
  2. More behind a node (decided): on 2 stappen a direct node with more second-step nodes than fit (2) shows "+n"; in the list for screen readers "nog n via …". Whether 2 per node becomes a setting is judged after use.
  3. Choosing a focus (decided): a sheet with a search field over items, people and projects; before typing what was opened last: items, people and projects (at most five, while the app runs). Alternative: only items, people and projects are reached through their own screens (Verbanden tonen).
  4. Depth at start (decided): 1 stap, the choice kept while the app runs (not stored). 1 stap is the calmer view; 2 stappen one tap away.
  5. Windows (decided): the graph in the list pane, the focus in the right pane, so the detail follows the focus. Alternative: the graph over both panes (more room, like Horizon en stilstand) and the detail only through Openen.
