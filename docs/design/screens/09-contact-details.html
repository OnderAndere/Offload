Behaviour and the open points

  • (a) Job title and organisation: one line under the name ("Voorzitter · Tennisclub Groenoord"), edited in a small dialog, as the birthday (proposal); the alternative is two text fields in the profile, as the note.
  • (b) Tapping a row dials or mails at once (proposal, step C); until step C is built, a tap opens Wijzigen…. On Windows a number is copied instead of dialled, with a message "Gekopieerd".
  • (c) Deleting only through ⋮ (and Del on Windows), with undo; no swipe (proposal).
  • (d) The label as chips in the sheet, one chosen or none; Eigen label… opens a text field; a new detail gets Mobiel for a number and no label for an address (proposal).
  • (e) Empty: the section header with the two add buttons is always there (proposal), so adding is never more than one tap; the alternative is one quiet line "Contactgegevens toevoegen".
  • Order: numbers first, then addresses, each in the order added. Changing the order (drag) is not in this step.
  • Screen readers (REQ-016): a row reads as "06-12 34 56 78, Mobiel, telefoonnummer"; ⋮ has a tooltip; the actions are also available as accessibility actions on the row.
  • Search: already in step A: a number (also by its digits), an address, an own label, the organisation and the job title find the person.
