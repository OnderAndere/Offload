Projects (T3.1) – sketch, screen 6

Indicative, not a pixel specification. Screen texts are Dutch, as in the app (app_nl.arb); colours follow DD-017. Already decided (DD-011, and PO 30-09-2026): Projecten is a destination; projects have at most two levels; a project screen shows its sub-projects first, then its own items grouped by status (Inbox, Actief, Wacht op, Gepland, Notities; Afgerond folded; archived items not shown); an item gets its project in the item detail and on the triage card (a chip, not a seventh destination); deleting a project keeps its items (they lose the project, with undo); a finished project can be archived; a fixed row Zonder project at the top of the list, and the temporary switch in the Inbox goes. Open for the PO: (1) the Windows layout: variant A or B; (2) whether cards elsewhere (Vandaag, Inbox, search) show the project as a chip; (3) the details marked with a yellow note. Today in the samples: Wednesday 30 September.
Decided (PO, 30-09-2026): Windows variant A; the project chip on cards elsewhere: yes; 3a plain capture; 3b sub-projects move to the top level (for now); 3c a parent archives its sub-projects; 3d names unique per parent, case-insensitive; 3e a parent counts its sub-projects (to be judged in use). See DESIGN.md, DD-018 point 6.

Behaviour (both variants) and the smaller questions

  • Two levels: a project is top level or directly under a top-level project. Verplaatsen… offers Bovenaan and the top-level projects; a project that has sub-projects cannot move under another one.
  • Names: unique among live, not archived projects with the same parent, case-insensitive; an empty name is refused. A clash that arises by itself (delete, undo, restore) gives a number: "Renovatie (2)".
  • Delete (with confirmation dialog and undo message): the project becomes a tombstone; its items keep existing and lose the project; its sub-projects move to the top level. Nothing is lost (question 3b).
  • Archive: the project leaves the list and the pickers, its items keep the link and stay findable by search; Gearchiveerd at the bottom of the list can be unfolded, and ⋮ there offers Terugzetten. Archiving a parent archives its sub-projects too.
  • Counts: "open" = every live item except Afgerond, Gearchiveerd and notes; a parent counts its sub-projects too. ⚠ counts open items with a deadline before today.
  • Zonder project opens like a project (same groups), without ⋮. The temporary switch "Ook verwerkte items tonen" in the Inbox goes (DD-018 point 1).
  • Keys on Windows in the project list: ↑/↓ and Enter opens; Ctrl+3 goes to Projecten. In a project the list keys of DD-018 point 5 apply (Del, Ctrl+Enter).
  • Question 3a (decided): capturing while a project is open is plain capture: to the inbox, without a project (REQ-001).
  • Later: ^project in the text (T10.5), the journal per project (REQ-057), the snapshot on a project (REQ-049).
