Idee: logboek per moestuin
3 d
  • Common to all variants: tapping a row opens the item detail (DD-018); the duplicate hint stays under the newer item of a pair (REQ-053); a date chip is orange with a warning icon only when the date is today or overdue, otherwise calm blue (DD-017); chips come from the stored tokens, never from a separate field (DD-004).
  • Relative time (PO decision): zojuist (< 1 min), 12 min, 1 u (hours), gisteren, 2 d … up to a week, then the date (ma 21 sep). The exact moment stays in the item detail. Text for screen readers written out ("12 minuten geleden").
  • Tokens in the text: A shows the text as typed with the tokens coloured (you see what you typed); B and C hide the tokens in the text and show them only as chips (calmer, but the text differs from what you typed).
  • Shared mail: A shows only the subject plus "+ 3 regels"; B shows subject plus two preview lines; C subject plus one line.
  • Later, not in this round: swiping to the trash (backlog); the triage card (screen 3) gets its own layout.
