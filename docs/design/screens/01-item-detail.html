Item detail (T2.3) – Q5b sketch

Two variants for opening and editing one item. Example: an item from the widget, still in the inbox. Indicative, not a pixel specification. Screen texts are Dutch, as in the app (app_nl.arb); colours follow DD-017 (blue = calm, orange = needs attention now). Decided (PO, 27-09-2026): variant A. The rules are in DESIGN.md, DD-018.

A · Text first — chosen

The typed text stays the source (DD-004). You edit it as in quick capture: chips, suggestions and the prefix bar. Deadline, people and tags follow from the text; picking one with a button inserts the token into the text. Only status and type are separate choices.

Decisions (PO, 27-09-2026) — details in DESIGN.md, DD-018

  1. Variant A, text first: one source, edited like quick capture.
  2. Autosave after a 2-second pause and when leaving the screen; no save button.
  3. No separate title field; lists show the first line.
  4. Status: one button for the next logical step (Afronden) plus Wachten op…; all statuses through the status chip.
  5. Trash in the ⋮ menu, with an undo message.
  6. Editing and tokens: the deadline follows the text; a date already in the text keeps its date, a newly typed relative date counts from the edit; a date picked with a button goes in as a fixed date.
  7. Enter adds a new line in the detail; a date chip is orange only for today or overdue.
