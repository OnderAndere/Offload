Indicative, not a pixel specification. Screen texts are Dutch, as in the app (app_nl.arb). Sample data is fictional; today is Friday 9 October 2026. Orange outline = new. Starting point (PO 06-10-2026, after use; DD-018 point 13 (e)): show the open "Waar was ik" notes on Vandaag, as a trial. The notes themselves, the form and the resume card in the item or project stay as built (T10.3). To decide: where on Vandaag (A, B or C), and the questions in the legend. The card is the same in A and B: a calm blue (not orange: a note is not late), the title of the item or project, the next step on one line (else what was in mind), and how long ago. A tap opens the item or project, where the resume card has Bijwerken and Hervat.

A · At the top, under the inbox line (advice)

Where you look first when you sit down again: what you were in the middle of. Above the deadlines, so kept short: at most 3, newest first; "+2 meer" unfolds the rest in place. A note on an item that is also due today shows in both places (a different question each).

  • Q1 · Where (A, B or C): advice A, at the top, at most 3 with "+n meer" to unfold in place.
  • Q2 · Which notes: every open note of a live item (not done, archived, parked with a future "Terugzien op", or in the trash) or a live project (not archived, not in the trash); newest first. Advice: no age limit, the age is shown, and Hervat in the item clears it.
  • Q3 · On the card: advice no buttons and no swipe on Vandaag: a tap opens the item or project, where Hervat and Bijwerken are. A swipe on Vandaag completes or trashes an item; on a note that would be confusing.
  • Q4 · A trial: advice no setting to switch it off; judged after use (M4b), like other trials.
  • Q5 · "Niets voor vandaag": notes are not deadlines; with only notes, the empty message still shows below them.
