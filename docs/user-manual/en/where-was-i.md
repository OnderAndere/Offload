# Where was I?

If you switch to something else, you often no longer know where you left off. With **Where was
I?** you record in a few seconds where you were with an item or a project: what the next step
is and what was on your mind. When you come back, it is at the top.

## Making a note

You find the **Where was I?** button (with a pause icon) in two places:

- in an item, next to **Complete** and **Wait for…**;
- in a project, under the title.

- **Windows:** **Ctrl+Shift+P** does the same, in an item or project. This also works if the
  item is only selected in the list and not opened yet.

A form opens with the title **Where was I?**, with below it the title of the item or project,
for example "Compare window quotes". The form has two fields:

- **Next step** — what you are going to do first, for example "Ask about the price of the
  second quote".
- **What I had in mind** — for example "why, what you wanted to check…". You see that as a hint
  in the field.

One filled-in field is enough.

- **Save** keeps the note.
- **Close** closes the form. What you typed is kept, even if you close the form in another way;
  if you open it again, it is still there.
- **Windows:** **Enter** saves, **Shift+Enter** starts a new line and **Esc** closes.
- **Android:** on the on-screen keyboard › goes to the second field and ✓ saves.

If you empty both fields and save, the note disappears, just like with **Resume**. If saving
fails, you see "Where was I? was not saved."

## The "Where was I" card

As long as a note is open, a card **Where was I** is at the top of the item or project. It
shows both fields and how long ago you saved the note.

- **Update** opens the form, already filled in, so that you can adjust it.
- **Resume** closes the note. The card then disappears.

The card stays until you choose **Resume**. There is one note at a time per item or project:
if you choose **Where was I?** or **Update** again, you update that note.

## Counts as touching

A saved note counts as touching the item. As a result it stays out of the **Stale** list in
the [Weekly review](weekly-review.md) and does not turn orange in **Standstill** under
[Insights](insights.md). **Resume** does not count as touching.

## On Today

Open notes are also at the top of [Today](today.md#where-was-i), newest first. If you tap such
a note, the item or project opens with the card **Where was I**. If you choose **Resume**
there, the note disappears from Today. You can turn this off in
[Settings](settings.md#today).
