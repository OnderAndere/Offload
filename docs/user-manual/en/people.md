# People

Under **People**, the fourth place in the navigation, is everyone you have mentioned: see
[Switching between Today, Inbox, Projects, People and Insights](navigating.md). On Windows you also
get there with **Ctrl+4**.

You do not create people with a button. They come into being when you mention someone with `@name`
in your text, see [Capturing something](capturing.md), or when you choose who asked for something
or who you are waiting for, while processing or in an item.

## The list of people

The list is a balance of what you owe people and what they owe you. Only open items count: items
that are not yet completed or archived.

At the top are two totals:

- **I still have to deliver** — for example "5 to 3 people": open items that someone asked of you.
- **I am still waiting for** — for example "2 from 2 people": open items with the status
  **Waiting** at a person.

Below that is a legend: **← I deliver**, **I wait →** and **oldest**. Below it is a row per person
with:

- the name;
- a bar from the middle. To the left, in orange, are the open items that the person asked of you
  (what you deliver); to the right, in blue, the items for which you are waiting on that person.
  Each part shows the number, so that the direction and the number tell the story and not only the
  colour;
- the age of that person's oldest open item, for example "25 d".

An age from the number of days at **Old after** (default 21, see [Settings](settings.md)) counts as
old: it is then shown in orange, bold and with a timer icon. So exactly that number of days or more
is old.

What Offload counts from: what you deliver from the day it was asked (or, if there is none, the day
you captured it); what you are waiting for from the day you captured it.

An item that a person asked for and for which you are waiting on the same person counts in both
parts.

The order: first the people with the most open items, in alphabetical order when equal; then the
people without open items, in grey. With the field **Find a person** you filter on name or
nickname; if Offload finds nobody, you see "Nobody found." A screen reader reads a row aloud as for
example "Jan: you deliver 3, you wait for 1, oldest 25 days, old".

- **Windows:** with **up arrow** and **down arrow** you move through the list; **Enter** opens the
  person on the right.

## Opening a person

Tap a person. The person's screen is your preparation for a one-to-one conversation. At the top is
the name and, if there are any, the other names by which you find them ("also: Jan, JJ"). Below
that are, in this order, the line for job title and organisation, directly below it the note, then
the birthday and your [contact details](#contact-details), see also
[Birthday and note](#birthday-and-note). After that come the lists, each with the number of
open items:

- **Requested by «name»** — items where this person is set as the one who asked for it.
- **Waiting for «name»** — items with the status **Waiting** at this person.
- **Mentioned in** — items in which you mention the person with `@` and which are not in either of
  the other two lists.

In **Requested by** and **Waiting for**, what is overdue or late comes first (the deadline or the
expected day has passed), and then the oldest. The age counts in the same way as in the balance:
what you deliver from the day it was asked, what you are waiting for from the day you captured it.

You do not see empty lists; if the person has nothing yet, you see "Nothing with this person yet."
Completed items appear per list under **Done · n**, collapsed; tap it to show them.

The cards are the same as elsewhere, including the status. Tap a card to open the item, see
[Viewing and editing an item](item-detail.md).

- **Android:** you go back with the arrow at the top left or your device's back button.
- **Windows:** in a wide window the balance stays on the left and the person opens on the right,
  next to it. With **Enter** in the list you go to the person; there you choose a card with **up
  arrow** and **down arrow** and **Enter** opens that card as its own screen. **Esc** closes that
  screen again, even if the focus is not in the text. **Esc** or **Backspace** (when you are not
  typing) closes the person and takes you back to the list. If you search in the meantime, the
  right-hand part shows the chosen item; the person comes back as soon as the search field is empty.
  In a narrow window the person opens over the list and **Esc** or **Backspace** goes back.

## Job title and organisation

Directly under the name is a line for what someone does and where. If nothing has been filled in
yet, you see **Add job title and organisation**. Tap the line for the window **Job title and
organisation** with two fields, **Job title** and **Organisation**. If everything is filled in, you
see for example "Team leader · Bakery De Korenaar". Save with **Save**, or choose **Cancel** to
change nothing.

## Contact details

Under the note and the birthday is the heading **Contact details**, with the person's phone numbers
and email addresses. You can keep more than one of each. Each detail has a label.

**Adding.** Choose **+ Phone number** or **+ Email address**. A window opens (**Add phone number**
or **Add email address**) with the keyboard that fits what you enter. Below it, choose a label:
**Mobile**, **Work**, **Private**, **Other** or **Own label…**. For a phone number, **Mobile** is
already selected. If you choose **Own label…**, you type it in the field **Label**. Save with
**Save** (on Windows Enter also works), or choose **Cancel**.

If what you typed does not fit, a message appears under the field: "This does not look like a phone
number.", "This does not look like an email address." or "Enter something." A phone number consists
of digits, spaces and the characters + ( ) . / -, with at least three digits. Offload keeps the
text as you typed it.

**From your text.** If you type a phone number or email address in an item and mention exactly one
person with `@` in it, it is added to that person as soon as you save the item, with **Undo**. See
[Phone numbers and email addresses](capturing.md#phone-numbers-and-email-addresses).

**Using.** Tap a detail:

- **Phone number, Android:** the phone app opens with the number filled in. No call is made; you
  do that yourself.
- **Phone number, Windows:** the number is copied. You see "Copied".
- **Email address:** your mail app starts a new message to that address. If there is no app that
  can do that, you see "No app found to open this."

**The menu (⋮) of a detail** has **Call** or **Mail** (where possible), **Copy**, **Change…** and
**Delete**. With **Delete** the message "Phone number deleted" or "Email address deleted" with
**Undo** appears at the bottom. If restoring fails, for example because the person has been merged
in the meantime, you see "The detail could not come back."

**Windows: with the keyboard.** If the focus is on a detail:

- **Enter** does the same as a tap.
- **Delete** deletes the detail, with the message to undo. A selected card in the lists below is
  never moved to the trash by this.
- **Shift+F10** opens the detail's menu; **Esc** closes it again.
- **Up arrow** and **down arrow** go to the detail above or below.

You also find a person by a phone number, email address, job title, organisation or own label, see
[Searching](searching.md).

## Birthday and note

**Birthday.** Under the note is a line with a cake. If the person has no birthday yet, you see
**Add birthday** (with a +). Tap the line for the window **Birthday**:

- **Day** — a number.
- **Month** — choose the month from the list.
- **Year (optional)** — leave empty if you do not know the year.

Choose **Save**, or **Cancel** to change nothing. If there is already a birthday, there is also
**Remove**. A date that does not exist gives "This date does not exist." Without a year you may
choose 29 February.

Without a year you see for example "April 3"; with a year "April 3, 1980 · 46 years", where the age
is as of today.

### Birthday notification

If a person has a birthday, Offload reminds you of it on the day itself, not earlier. This happens
in two places:

- **A notification** at the time of the day notification (default 09:00, can be changed under
  [Settings](settings.md#reminders)), on Android and on Windows as long as Offload is running.
  For one person it says for example "Anna de Vries has a birthday today", and if you know the year,
  below it "turns 46". If there are more, it says "2 birthdays today" with the names (and ages). If
  you tap the notification, the person opens (on Windows in the right-hand panel), or for several
  people **Today**. On Android's lock screen it only says "Birthday today" or the number, never a
  name.
- **A line on [Today](today.md#birthdays)**.

If someone's birthday is on 29 February, the reminder comes on 28 February in years without a 29
February, with the correct age. If you do not want them, switch off **Birthdays** under
**Settings** → **Reminders**.

**Note.** Directly under the line for job title and organisation is the field **Note** ("What you
want to remember about this person"). What you type is saved automatically, about two seconds after
you stop typing and also when you leave the screen. If saving fails, the field says: "Saving
failed. Your change is still there; please try again." For now the note is plain text. With
[Searching](searching.md) you also find a person by what is in the note.

## Renaming a person

A person's menu (⋮) has **Rename**. The window **Rename person** asks for a new **Name** and says
that the old name keeps working as a nickname: if someone was called `Jan`, `@Jan` in earlier and
new items still points to this person.

- An empty name gives "Enter a name."
- If the name already belongs to another person (or is a nickname of that person), you see "There is
  already a person with that name." That window then also has the button **Merge with «name»…**; it
  leads to the same confirmation as below.

## Merging two people

Is the same person in your list twice, for example as `Jan` and as `Jan de Vries`? Then you merge
them.

1. Open one of the two and choose **Merge with…** in the menu (⋮).
2. In the list **Merge «name» with** are all other people. Search by name or nickname with the field
   **Find a person**. If one is left, **Enter** chooses it. If there is nobody else, you see "There
   is nobody else yet."
3. You get the question **Merge people?**: "Merge «A» with «B»? All items and nicknames go to «B»;
   "«A»" keeps working as a nickname." Choose **Merge**, or **Cancel** to do nothing.

After that you see the screen of the person you chose («B»). They keep their name. Everything from
«A» goes to «B»: the items that «A» asked for or for which you are waiting on «A», and also every
`@«A»` in your texts, which now counts for «B». The name and the nicknames of «A» become nicknames
of «B», each one once.

The notes of both people follow each other in «B»'s note. A birthday is kept: if both people have
one, the one of the person you chose applies; if that person has none, they get the one of «A». The
contact details of «A» also go to «B»; a detail that «B» already has is not added twice. With
**Undo** they come back to «A».

At the bottom is the message "«A» was merged with «B»" with **Undo**. With it you undo the merge in
one go. If that fails, you see "Undo failed; the people stay merged."

## Who asked for this and who are you waiting for

In an item, next to the chips for status, type and project, are two chips for people, see
[Viewing and editing an item](item-detail.md):

- ✋ **Who asked for this?** — appears as soon as a person is set or the type is **Request**.
- ⏳ **Who are you waiting for?** — appears as long as the status is **Waiting**, even without a
  person.

Tap such a chip for a menu: **Open «name»** (opens the person), **Other person…** and **Remove**.
Without a person only **Other person…** is in the menu.

The window **Who asked for this?** or **Who are you waiting for?** first shows the people you
mentioned with `@` in the item's text, with "in the text" behind them, and below them the other
people. You can also type a new name. Via **Wait for…** (or **Waiting** in the status chip),
**Skip** means: the item starts waiting without you choosing anyone. With **Other person…**,
**Skip** changes nothing. If you close the window, nothing changes either. See also
[Processing your inbox](triage.md).

## From the text to the person

Under an item's text, every `@name` appears as a chip. Tap it to open that person.

- **Android:** the person opens over the item; going back brings you to the item.
- **Windows:** in a wide window the person opens on the right, see
  [Opening a person](#opening-a-person).

## People on cards and in search results

On **Today**, in the **Inbox**, in a project and in search results, a card shows the chips ✋ and ⏳
with the person's name, unless the text already mentions that person with `@`. In search results, a
tap on a person opens that person's screen, see [Searching](searching.md).
