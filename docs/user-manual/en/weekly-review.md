# Weekly review

The **Weekly review** goes through what needs attention in five steps: first a look at your
**Insights**, then your inbox, what you are waiting for, your deadlines and what has stalled. You
do it whenever you want. If it has been a while, a line on **Today** points it out to you.

## Starting

- Tap the checklist icon at the top of **Today** (tooltip **Weekly review**), or the line
  **Weekly review · last 9 d ago** (or **· not done yet**) if it is there, see [Today](today.md).
- **Windows:** **Ctrl+Shift+W**. This does not work while you are processing, see
  [Processing your inbox](triage.md).

On **Windows** the review appears in the list column and an open item stays on the right. On
**Android** it is a screen of its own. With **✕** (tooltip **Close the weekly review**) you close
it at any moment.

## The start screen

The start screen shows the five steps with what is there now:

1. **Insights** — "What is coming, what has stalled, where you stand with whom".
2. **Empty the inbox** — for example "3 items to process".
3. **Go through waiting for** — the number of items.
4. **Deadlines** — for example "2 overdue · 4 in the next 2 weeks".
5. **Stale** — for example "5 items, untouched for 3 weeks or more".

A step with nothing to do shows "✓ nothing to do" and is skipped; **Insights** is always there.
Tap **Start** to begin; you then arrive at **Insights**.

## Going through the steps

Every step shows the title at the top, "1 / 5" and a progress bar. With **Previous** and **Next**
you go back and forth; at the last step it says **Finish**. After **Insights**, the button
**Continue** appears instead of **Next**.

### Step 1: Insights

Here you see the same **Horizon**, **Standstill** and **People** as on the **Insights** tab, see
[Insights](insights.md). You only look; there is nothing to handle here. In this step
**Standstill** does not show the links **To the weekly review →** and **Open project →**. On
**Windows**, **Horizon** and **Standstill** are side by side.

### Step 2: Empty the inbox

You see how many items are in your inbox and the button **Process**. It starts processing your
inbox, see [Processing your inbox](triage.md); afterwards you come back to the review. If the inbox
is empty, it says "✓ The inbox is empty".

### Step 3: Go through waiting for

Here are the items with the status **Waiting**, the longest untouched at the top, with for example
"untouched for 2 weeks". On each card you choose:

- **Still waiting** — you have looked and are still waiting. The item then no longer counts as
  stale.
- **Complete** — sets the item to **Done**, with **Undo**.
- the person icon, **Other person…** — choose who you are waiting for, see [People](people.md).

A handled card stays, shown lighter, with "✓ Still waiting" or "✓ Done".

### Step 4: Deadlines

Items that are overdue and items with a deadline up to 14 days ahead, under **Overdue** and
**Next 2 weeks**, as on **Today**. Tap a card to open the item, see
[Viewing and editing an item](item-detail.md).

- **Android:** swipe right to complete, left for the trash, with **Undo**.

### Step 5: Stale

Open items, including notes, that have not been touched for a while, the oldest at the top. The
heading says, for example, "Untouched for 3 weeks or longer · 5". From 6 weeks on, the age is shown
in orange. On each card you choose:

- **Keep** — the item stays, and then no longer counts as stale.
- **Later…** — choose a day in **Revisit on**, see [Parking an item](revisit.md). The item then no
  longer counts as stale.
- **Archive** — with **Undo**.
- the trash icon — with **Undo**.

"Touched" means: changed. Merely opening an item does not count. Handled cards stay with "✓ Kept",
"✓ Back on Mon, Oct 12", "✓ Archived" or "✓ In the trash".

## The end screen

After the last step you see **Done ✓**, with what you did: "n still waiting", "n kept", "n done",
"n to revisit later", "n archived" and "n in the trash". If you did nothing, it says "Everything
was up to date." With **To Today** you go back.

## When a review is due

A review counts as done as soon as you see the end screen **Done ✓**, even if there was nothing to
do in any step. If you close halfway with **✕**, it does not count. Offload remembers that moment
per device: a review on your phone therefore does not remove the line on your PC. The review is due
again if you have never done it, or if the last review is at least as many days ago as you have
set.

## A reminder

Under **Settings** → **Reminders** you turn on **Weekly review reminder**, and choose the **Day**
(Friday by default) and the **Time** (16:00 by default). The switch is off by default.

On that day and at that time the notification **Weekly review due** comes, with below it "Last time
9 days ago" or "Not done yet". You only get it when the review is due, by the same rule as the line
on **Today** (see above). If you did the review on time, no notification comes. If it stays due,
one notification comes on each chosen day; last week's disappears then, so there is never more
than one.

A tap (Android) or click (Windows) on the notification opens the start screen of the weekly
review. It is a separate notification next to the one for deadlines.

> **Android:** if Android bundles several notifications from Offload into one group, a tap on that
> group only opens the app. Expand the group and tap the notification itself.

**Windows:** the reminder comes as a Windows notification as long as Offload is running, also in
the system tray. After **Quit** or a restart of the PC it does not come until you open Offload
again. See [Notifications on Windows](today.md#notifications-on-windows).

## Settings

Under **Settings** → **Weekly review** you choose:

- **Due again after** — after how many days (3, 5, 7, 10, 14, 21 or 28) the line on **Today**
  appears. The default is 7 days.
- **Stale after** — after how many weeks (1 to 12) an open item comes up in step 5. The default is
  3 weeks.

Both apply per device, see [Settings](settings.md).

## Keyboard shortcuts (Windows)

While you are in a step and no text field has focus:

- **↑** and **↓** — choose a card.
- **Enter** — opens the chosen item on the right.
- **1** — **Still waiting** (step 3) or **Keep** (step 5).
- **2** — **Archive** (step 5).
- **3** — **Later…** (step 5).
- **Ctrl+Enter** — **Complete** (steps 3 and 4).
- **Delete** — to the trash.
- **→** and **←** — next and previous step.
- **Esc** — stops the review; from the opened item you first go back to the list.

During the review, **Ctrl+1** to **Ctrl+5** and **Ctrl+F** do nothing and the search field is off.
If you click a place in the bar on the left, the review stops.
