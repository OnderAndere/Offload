# Offload — user manual

Offload is a personal, fully offline app for quickly capturing ongoing work, future work, ideas
and requests from others, on Android and Windows 11. Everything stays on your own device,
encrypted and without internet.

## Getting started

- [Starting Offload](starting.md): opening the app, your encrypted data, Offload on Windows in
  the system tray.
- [Finding your way](navigating.md): Today, Inbox, Projects, People and Insights; on Windows the
  side-by-side view and keyboard shortcuts.

## Capturing

- [Capturing a thought](capturing.md): the **Capture** button, the widgets, the share menu, the
  Windows keyboard shortcut, and the signs `@` `#` `!` `^` `*` that add a person, tag, date,
  project or priority while you type.

## Processing

- [Processing your inbox](triage.md): card by card, give each item its place.
- [Viewing and editing an item](item-detail.md): status, type, text, note, photos, sharing, completing.
- [Recurring tasks](recurring.md): a task that comes back daily, weekly or monthly.
- [Parking an item](revisit.md): out of sight until the day you choose.

## Keeping an overview

- [Today](today.md): what is due today or overdue, and what needs your attention now.
- [Where was I](where-was-i.md): note your next step before you switch tasks.
- [Weekly review](weekly-review.md): five steps to go through everything once a week.
- [Insights](insights.md): what is coming, what has stalled, and how things are connected.

## Finding things back

- [Searching](searching.md): items and people, best match first.
- [Projects](projects.md): grouping items into projects and sub-projects.
- [People](people.md): what you owe and what you are waiting for, per person.

## Settings and your data

- [Settings](settings.md): language, input, reminders, models, backup and export, storage.
