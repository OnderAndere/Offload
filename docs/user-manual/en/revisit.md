# Parking an item until a day (Revisit on)

Do you want to not see an item for a while, but not lose it? Then park it until a day. On that
day it automatically appears in your inbox again.

## Parking an item

Open the item and choose **Revisit on…** in the menu (⋮). Or tap the status chip and choose
**Scheduled**: that always asks first on which day you want to see the item again.

A choice list **Revisit on** opens with:

- **Tomorrow**;
- **Next week** — the next Monday. If today is Monday, it is the Monday after. If it is
  Sunday, it is the Sunday a week later (so not tomorrow);
- **In a month** — the same day next month, or the last day of that month if that day does not
  exist;
- **Choose date…** — opens a calendar, starting from tomorrow.

If the item is already parked, there is also **Not any more**: the item then becomes
**Active** again.

**Windows:** the choice list is a small window. Choose with **↑**/**↓** and **Enter**; **Esc**
closes it.

## After parking

- The item gets the status **Scheduled**.
- You see the message "Back on Mon, Oct 12" (with the date you chose) with **Undo**.
- In the item, under the status, is the line "Revisit on Mon, Oct 12". Tap it to choose a
  day again; with ✕ you choose **Not any more**.
- On cards in projects, people and search results there is a chip with a refresh icon and the
  day.
- A parked item does not count as stale in the [Weekly review](weekly-review.md) and does not
  appear in **Standstill**, see [Insights](insights.md).
- If you change the status of a parked item yourself, the revisit lapses.

You can also park while [processing your inbox](triage.md) (button **Later…**) and in the
[Weekly review](weekly-review.md) (step **Stale**).

## On the day itself

From the start of that day the item is in your **Inbox** again. This happens when the app
starts, when it comes back to the front, or when the day changes while the app is open. On
[Today](today.md) it is in the group **Back in your inbox**, until you have processed it.

## Notification

On the day itself a notification comes, in the same single notification per day as for
deadlines, see [Notifications](today.md#notifications-for-deadlines). If there is only returned
work: **Back in your inbox** with the title of the item, or "3 back in your inbox". If there
are also deadlines, it says for example "1 deadline · 2 back in your inbox", with the deadlines
first and then lines such as "Back: title". If you tap the notification, the item opens (for
one item) or **Today**.

**Android:** if Android has not yet asked for permission for notifications, that happens as
soon as you park your first item.

**Windows:** the notification comes as a Windows notification as long as Offload is running,
also in the system tray. A click opens the item or **Today**. After **Quit** no notification
comes; the item is then in your inbox. See [Notifications on Windows](today.md#notifications-on-windows).
